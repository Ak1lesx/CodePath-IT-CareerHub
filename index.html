How to Start Your IT Career

  1. Pick a path: support, development, data, cloud, security or business and design.
  2. Build skills with free courses, certifications and small personal projects.
  3. Put your projects on GitHub and prepare a clean resume and portfolio.
  4. Apply to entry-level roles and internships, then practice for interviews.
